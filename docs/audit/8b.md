# 8b: deterministic §14.2 draft lint (ADR-026)

Branch `feat/phase-8b-lint`, stacked on #51. Owner choice: built-in data lists
(option a, the recommended one); no downloaded blocklist.

## What changed

* `lint.py` is a pure `lint(LintInput) -> tuple[Finding, ...]`. Each `Finding`
  has a stable `code`, a `level` (`block` or `warn`) and a message saying
  exactly what to fix. Blocking findings sort first.
* **Block (§14.2):**
  * `subject_empty`, `body_empty`;
  * `recipient_unverified`;
  * `sender_noreply`, `sender_name_missing` (used once Phase 9 connects an
    account);
  * `subject_reply_prefix`, `subject_all_caps`;
  * `html_not_allowed` (covers tracking pixels, images and hidden text);
  * `too_many_links` (more than 2), `url_shortener`, `redirect_link`;
  * `spam_phrase`, `duplicate_body`, `no_specific_reference` (the body must
    still mention a verified paper title or topic);
  * `too_many_attachments` (more than 1), `attachment_type` (CV only).
* **Warn:** `subject_long` (over 78), `exclamations` (more than 1),
  `excessive_caps` (over 30% shouted words), `body_long` (over 350 words).
* Data lists: `SPAM_PHRASES`, `SHORTENER_HOSTS`, `REDIRECT_HOSTS`,
  `REDIRECT_PARAMS`, `REDIRECT_PATHS`, `ALLOWED_ATTACHMENT_KINDS`.
* `drafts.lint_draft()` lints the current version with one indexed
  duplicate lookup plus the references taken from the version's evidence.
* QA n1 on #51: falling back to the newest paper or the first topic adds a
  flag.
* QA n4 on #51: CV evidence says "your original CV" or "your CV approved for
  this professor".

## QA checklist

* Stable codes, with hard and soft levels separated (tested per rule: 18 block
  and 4 warn cases).
* The lists are data, not scattered regexes. The spam phrases compile into one
  word-bounded alternation (tested against false positives such as
  "unguaranteedness").
* Pure and deterministic, with no network (`socket.connect` is patched to
  fail; a repeat gives an identical result).
* One pass: each regex runs once over the subject plus body. A 10 KB body
  lints in well under 0.2 s (tested).
* Messages name the fix ("Remove 'RE:' or 'FWD:' — this is a first contact.",
  the spam phrases found, the link host).

## Five priorities

* **Latency:** linear in the text; one indexed query in `lint_draft`.
* **Data structures:** `frozenset` host lookups, one compiled alternation for
  the phrases, and links parsed once.
* **Security:** HTML is rejected outright, along with shorteners and
  redirectors; the recipient must be verified; the attachment is CV only.
* **UX:** each message tells the student what to change; style issues are
  only warnings.
* **Cost:** no network, no dependency, no LLM.
